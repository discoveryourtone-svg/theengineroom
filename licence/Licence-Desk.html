Licence Desk.

Issue licence keys to your pre-sale buyers. Everything happens on this computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Step 1

Your signing key.

The private key file that proves a licence came from you. Load it each time you open this page.

Drop engine-room-private.jwk here, or
First time? Create your signing key
Do this once, before you issue any real keys. You'll get two files: engine-room-private.jwk (keep secret, back it up in two places, never email or upload it) and LicencePublicKey.h (replace the one in the plugin's Source folder, then rebuild). Making a new key later switches off every licence you've already issued.

The record of every key you've issued. Load your latest copy so founder numbers carry on and nobody gets two keys. Skip this only for your very first batch.
